# Cookie Quest: The Golden Crumb

A three-level platform game: pick a mouse (Crumb, Princess Pearl or Baby Bean), collect cookies, and reach the Golden Cookie at the finish line. It's worth 1,000 points.

**Play:** https://gitjonbeb.github.io/cookie-quest/

- **Tablet or phone:** hold the arrow buttons to run and tap JUMP; hold JUMP to jump higher. Turn the tablet sideways. Tip: use "Add to Home Screen" to get a full-screen app icon.
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys or A/D to move, Space to jump, Esc to pause.
- **Xbox controller:** stick or d-pad to move, A to jump, Start to pause.

Everything is in `index.html`: original art drawn in code and original music synthesized in the browser. There are no downloads, no ads and no data collection.
